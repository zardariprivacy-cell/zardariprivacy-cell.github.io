About FactTrench

FactTrench publishes one short, true, amazing fact-story every day — mostly from the deep ocean, the strangest place on Earth, and always checked against public scientific sources such as NOAA, universities and research journals.

We are the sister project of Trench Borne, a YouTube channel about deep-sea creatures. Read a fact here, then watch it move on the channel.

How we check facts

Every number on this site — depths, sizes, records — comes from a published scientific source. If scientists are still debating something, we say so. No invented records, ever.
