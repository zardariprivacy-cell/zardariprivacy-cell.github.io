About ScriptLens

ScriptLens is a free AI detector built for people who work with words — YouTubers checking scripts, teachers skimming essays, editors reviewing articles, and anyone who has ever read a paragraph and thought: a machine wrote this, didn't it?

Why we show the evidence

Most AI detectors hand you a single number and ask you to trust it. We think that is backwards. ScriptLens breaks its estimate into seven visible signals — sentence rhythm, AI-typical phrases, transition stuffing, contractions, personal voice, repetitive openings and word freshness — and colours your text sentence by sentence, so you can see exactly what the machine sees and decide for yourself.

Private by design

There is no server behind ScriptLens. The whole analysis runs as JavaScript inside your browser tab, which means the text you paste never leaves your device. No accounts, no uploads, no database, nothing to leak.

Honest about limits

No AI detector is 100% accurate, and anyone who claims otherwise is selling something. Formal human writing can score as AI; well-edited AI writing can score as human. Use ScriptLens as a strong, transparent hint — never as sole proof against a writer.

ScriptLens is a sister project of FactTrench, our daily amazing-facts blog, and the Trench Borne YouTube channel.
