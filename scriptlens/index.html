How ScriptLens works

1. You paste the text

A script, essay, article, caption — anything. Analysis happens entirely on your device, in this page. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared.

2. We read the rhythm

AI writing has a fingerprint: even sentence lengths, polished transitions, favourite phrases, almost no contractions or personal voice. Human writing is messier — and that mess is measurable.

3. You get the split

Seven signals combine into one estimate of AI-written versus human-written share, with the evidence shown sentence by sentence — never just a bare number.

The seven signals

Sentence rhythm (burstiness)

Humans write a long sentence, then three short ones. AI tends to produce sentences of suspiciously similar length, one after another.

AI's favourite phrases

"Delve", "furthermore", "in today's fast-paced world", "plays a crucial role"… Language models lean on the same polished phrases again and again.

Signpost stuffing

Moreover, additionally, in conclusion — heavy use of formal transition words is a classic machine-polish pattern.

Contractions

People write "don't", "it's", "we're". AI defaults to the full, formal "do not", "it is", "we are" far more often.

Personal voice

I, you, we — plus questions, asides and opinions. Text that talks like a person usually is one.

Repetitive openings

When sentence after sentence starts with "The…", "It…", "This…", a template is probably doing the writing.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this AI detector?

It is an estimate, not a verdict. ScriptLens reads the same surface patterns most detectors start from, and it is good at spotting unedited, copy-pasted AI text. But heavily edited AI text can look human, and very formal human writing (legal, academic) can look machine-made. Never use any detector's score alone to accuse someone — use it as a signal, then read the text yourself.

Can it detect ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini text?

It detects the patterns those tools share — even rhythm, polished transitions, favourite phrases — rather than any one tool's watermark. Fresh, unedited output from any major AI writer scores high; the more a human edits, the more human it reads.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

No. The analysis runs 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Close the tab and the text is gone. There is no server, no account and no database behind this page.

Why did my own writing score as AI?

Formal, careful writing — essays, reports, non-native English written very correctly — often shares patterns with AI output: even sentences, few contractions, formal transitions. A high score means "this reads polished and regular", not "you cheated".

How much text should I paste?

At least 50 words for a meaningful reading; 150–1,000 words is the sweet spot. Very short texts do not carry enough rhythm to judge.

Honest note: no AI detector on Earth is 100% accurate — including this one. ScriptLens gives you a transparent estimate and shows its evidence, so you can judge for yourself. Treat the score as a strong hint, never as proof.
