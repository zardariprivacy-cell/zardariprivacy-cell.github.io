Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your text stays on your device

ScriptLens is a static website with no server-side processing. When you paste text into the detector, the analysis runs entirely in your own browser using JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded, transmitted, stored, logged or shared. When you close the tab, it is gone.

What we collect

We do not operate accounts and we do not collect names, email addresses, passwords or payment details. We do not set tracking cookies of our own. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising

If advertising is shown on this site in the future, third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. This policy will be updated before any such change takes effect.

External links

This site links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on our sister site, FactTrench.
